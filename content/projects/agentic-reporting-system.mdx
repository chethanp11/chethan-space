## Problem statement

Reporting teams spend too much time translating repeated business questions into fragile one-off analysis.

## Architecture overview

The system separates intent capture, metric resolution, query generation, narrative synthesis, and approval.

## Capabilities

- Business question intake
- Metric and dimension grounding
- Draft narrative generation
- Analyst review workflow
- Audit log for published outputs

## Governance approach

The workflow never treats generated analysis as final without source attribution, metric definitions, and review state.
