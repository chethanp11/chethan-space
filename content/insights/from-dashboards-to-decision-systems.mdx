A dashboard is only one surface of an analytics system. The deeper question is whether the information changes decisions.

## What dashboards often miss

Many dashboards show activity without clarifying ownership, thresholds, escalation paths, or expected action. Teams then consume data passively instead of using it to run the business.

## Decision-system design

A decision system connects metrics to:

- A specific business question
- An accountable owner
- A review cadence
- An action threshold
- A feedback loop

## Leadership implication

Analytics leaders should measure success by the quality of decisions improved, not only the number of reports delivered.
