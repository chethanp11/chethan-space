Human-in-the-loop should not be a vague safety phrase. It should be a concrete workflow design.

## What good review includes

Reviewers need context, source evidence, confidence signals, and clear choices. They also need to know what they are accountable for approving.

## Design questions

- What can the system do automatically?
- What requires human judgment?
- What evidence must be shown?
- When should work be escalated?
- How are review decisions logged?

## Why it matters

Human review adds value when it is structured. Otherwise it becomes a bottleneck or a false sense of control.
