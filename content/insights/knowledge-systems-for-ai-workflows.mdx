AI systems depend on knowledge quality. If the knowledge layer is fragmented, stale, or unsourced, the workflow will inherit those weaknesses.

## Knowledge as infrastructure

Useful knowledge systems capture definitions, decisions, policies, examples, and source references. They also show freshness and ownership so users can understand reliability.

## Practical pattern

- Keep authoritative sources visible.
- Make review and update workflows explicit.
- Separate stable knowledge from temporary context.
- Reuse knowledge across multiple workflows.

## The payoff

When knowledge becomes infrastructure, AI work becomes less dependent on individual memory and more grounded in shared understanding.
