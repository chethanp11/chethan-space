AML analytics is not just a modeling problem. It is an accountable operating system where data, scenarios, thresholds, alerts, investigations, and regulatory expectations must connect.

## Why explainability matters

Teams need to understand why an alert fired, which data elements contributed, what changed over time, and whether the outcome can be defended. A model that cannot be explained creates friction for investigators and risk for the institution.

## Better operating pattern

- Keep lineage visible from source data to alert decision.
- Document scenario intent and threshold logic.
- Compare model performance with investigator feedback.
- Treat governance as part of delivery, not an afterthought.

## The practical goal

Scale matters, but trusted scale matters more. AML analytics should help teams focus attention without weakening accountability.
