AI transformation is often introduced through tools. Tools matter, but operating model design determines whether adoption becomes safe and useful.

## What needs to change

Teams need use-case intake, prioritization, risk review, data access patterns, evaluation methods, and support models. Without these, pilots remain disconnected from daily work.

## A practical sequence

1. Start with business workflows, not technology demos.
2. Define the decision or task being improved.
3. Add controls, review, and measurement.
4. Teach teams how to use the system responsibly.

## The outcome

Successful AI transformation feels less like a one-time launch and more like a new organizational capability.
